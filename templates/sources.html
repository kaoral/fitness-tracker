How your data is presented

Consistent units and honest gaps.

Dates and times use Europe/Berlin, including daylight saving time. Workout distances are shown in kilometres and durations as active time.

Missing measurements are shown as unavailable. Valid zero measurements are retained. Heart rate summaries use valid measurements from the available records.

Daily totals include only recorded data. Sparse coverage can affect averages and totals.

Refresh your snapshot

Your website reads the current export.

  1. Export a fresh database from Gadgetbridge.
  2. Stop the local website and replace instance/Gadgetbridge.db.
  3. Restart the website to explore the updated records.

Keep a backup of your exports. The website opens the source database in read-only mode.
